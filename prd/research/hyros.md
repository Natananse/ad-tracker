# HYROS for a Shopify merchant

Reviewed 18 November 2026 using public HYROS product pages, release notes, API documentation and terms. For a small Shopify store, the closest comparison is paid-ad attribution with Shopify sales and refunds. HYROS also sells to businesses that close sales through calls, courses and subscriptions. [HYROS product overview](https://hyros.com/updates/what-is-hyros/)

## From click to campaign report

1. **Connect the store.** HYROS says its Shopify app installs its tracking script and syncs sales through the Shopify API. The merchant can then compare orders from the store with traffic observed on the storefront. Its integration announcement covers all Shopify plans. [Shopify integration release](https://hyros.com/updates/changelog/version-2-1-5/)
2. **Connect an interaction to a sale.** HYROS describes collecting source information when someone follows an ad, recording later interactions, and linking the transaction using available identifiers such as an email address. First-touch and last-touch views let the merchant examine which interaction received credit. The identity rules and match rate for any particular store require a hands-on test. [HYROS mechanism](https://hyros.com/updates/what-is-hyros/)
3. **Adjust the campaign when money comes back.** HYROS says a Shopify refund updates the revenue credited to the original campaign. It also describes product-level reporting. Both capabilities belong in the competitive baseline for a refund-aware product. [HYROS Shopify refund and product reporting](https://hyros.com/updates/dropshipping-ad-tracking/)
4. **Feed results back to ad platforms.** HYROS advertises sending conversion data to Meta and Google and exposing attribution through Claude. An upload, the credit assigned by the platform, and a new sale are three different outcomes. A merchant would need a controlled comparison to measure additional sales. [HYROS product demonstration](https://hyros.com/)

## Two opportunities for the proposed product

- **Make a late refund easy to reconcile.** The HYROS API describes its `hyros_get_sales` refund-state filter as selecting sales by their sale date. A merchant who filters November sales can miss an October purchase refunded in November. Offer both a November adjustments report and an October campaign total restated for that refund. Test with a $100 October order and a $30 November merchandise refund. The merchant should find the same $30 adjustment in November and $70 of net merchandise revenue against the October order. HYROS also exposes a refund date through its sale-refunded webhook, so an integration can build this view. Whether the HYROS dashboard already offers it remains open. In the reference, see MCP → Sales and orders and Webhooks → Sale Refunded. [HYROS API documentation](https://api-docs.hyros.com/)
- **Let the owner price the app before a call.** Publish a cancellable monthly rate per order band with the included stores, connectors and behavior above the limit. In pricing interviews, give merchants their own previous month's order count and see whether they can state next month's charge unaided. Compare their answer with a written HYROS quote for the same store and term before estimating savings.
