# Triple Whale

**Public-source check, November 18, 2026.** A Shopify merchant can already get first- and last-click reporting from Triple Whale for free. Its paid suite also lets teams investigate campaigns down to individual orders. The opportunity to test is whether a smaller store will pay for a faster weekly decision about contribution after refunds, costs and ad spend. [Pricing and feature comparison](https://www.triplewhale.com/pricing) · [Order and journey drilldown](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/5960325-how-the-triple-pixel-works)

## How tracking and reporting work

The pixel observes site visits after installation. Campaign parameters identify the originating ad. Merchants can inspect return on ad spend (ROAS) by campaign, ad set or ad, open the associated orders, and inspect a customer's marketing journey and survey answer. Incorrect tagging or a late installation leaves gaps in that history. Triple Whale also identifies blocked or uninstalled pixels, interrupted checkout journeys, offline orders and third-party imports as reasons a purchase can lack attribution. [Triple Pixel](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/5960325-how-the-triple-pixel-works) · [Non-attributed conversions](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/11570583-understanding-non-attributed-conversions)

The model matters. Triple Attribution can give each contributing platform full credit, so its cross-platform attributed total can exceed the store's sales. First-click and last-click each select one interaction. Total Impact combines observed interactions and survey evidence, requires sufficient survey coverage, and uses a fixed seven-day window. The blended estimate answers a different question from counting eligible orders for reconciliation. [Attribution models](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/5960333-understanding-and-utilizing-attribution-models) · [Total Impact](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/7128379-the-total-impact-attribution-model)

The cost tools already support historical changes and returned-product costs. Triple Whale illustrates an order and a partial refund on different dates, with the cost adjustment reflected on the refund date. A comparison of our proposed contribution report should include this existing capability. [Cost of goods guide](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/6953395-set-your-cogs)

## Documented survey limits and alternatives

| Merchant task | What the native Triple Whale survey does | Existing alternative or proposed test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use an answer to send a follow-up or segment an order in Shopify | Responses stay in Triple Whale. Its native survey does not populate Shopify order tags, metafields or Flow triggers. It supports CSV export and integrations with separate survey providers. [Native survey FAQ](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/6486354-post-purchase-survey-pps-for-shopify-stores) | Fairing already syncs answers to Shopify order metafields. KnoCommerce already provides a new-survey-response Flow trigger. Ask which separate subscription, export or manual step an integrated product would replace. [Fairing documentation](https://docs.fairing.co/docs/shopify-analytics) · [KnoCommerce documentation](https://docs.knocommerce.com/en/articles/10629828-shopify-flow-integration) |
| Let a shopper skip a free-text follow-up | Progress past an open-ended question requires text. Earlier answers survive abandonment. [Native survey guide](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/6486354-post-purchase-survey-pps-for-shopify-stores) | Test an explicit skip control. Measure completion of subsequent questions and retention of the initial attribution answer. |
| Localize every visible survey control | Some default labels and confirmation text are locked even when the main question is translated. [Native survey FAQ](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/6486354-post-purchase-survey-pps-for-shopify-stores) | Have a multilingual merchant verify every customer-facing label in their own checkout before prioritizing full localization. |
| Compare imported and newly collected responses in one report | The native Survey Report shows live on-site responses. Historical imports remain accessible via attribution, the SQL editor and Moby. [Survey reporting guide](https://kb.triplewhale.com/en/articles/6486354-post-purchase-survey-pps-for-shopify-stores) | If migration matters to pilots, prototype one view with source and import date on each response. |

## What would earn a switch

**Proposed test.** Give a merchant one week's campaign spend, five real orders and one partial refund. Ask them to explain the difference between Shopify net merchandise revenue and campaign-attributed revenue, and decide whether the campaign still covers its variable costs. Compare task time and errors against their existing process. Include Shopify's built-in marketing reports and Triple Whale Free as baselines. Shopify already offers first-click and last-non-direct-click reporting. [Shopify marketing reports](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/marketing-reports) · [Triple Whale Free](https://www.triplewhale.com/pricing)

**Commercial question.** After the comparison, show the proposed monthly price for the merchant's actual order and event volume. Record which subscription or reconciliation task it replaces and whether the merchant will start a paid pilot. Simple attribution, survey write-back and transparent pricing each face existing alternatives. Willingness to pay for their combination remains untested.
